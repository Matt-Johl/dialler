The Application and the Server

The Application sends no personal information to Eccentric Badger. It connects only to the Server, which the organisation that provides you with the Application installs and runs on its own infrastructure. The Application contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Stored on your device

  • Your enrolment: the Server address, your extension, a device credential (kept in the iOS keychain) and the Server’s certificate fingerprint.
  • Your directory and favourites.
  • Your call history.
  • The Wi-Fi networks on which the device receives calls.

Signing out removes your enrolment, directory and Wi-Fi settings. Deleting the Application removes everything, including call history.

Sent to your organisation’s Server

  • Calls: your device identifier and extension, the numbers you call and that call you, and call audio. Audio is encrypted and relayed to the other party; the Application does not record calls.
  • Directory: changes you make to your contacts and favourites.
  • Diagnostics: Application logs and crash and performance reports from iOS, sent automatically when the Application starts. Credentials are removed from logs before sending. The Server keeps diagnostics for 30 days by default.

Every connection is encrypted and made only to your organisation’s Server, whose certificate the Application checks against the one it recorded at enrolment.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to carry your voice on calls.
  • Camera: to read the enrolment QR code. No images are stored or sent.
  • Local network: to reach your organisation’s Server.

Demo mode

In demo mode, calls are simulated on the device and nothing is sent anywhere.

The Server

The Server holds each device’s extension and label, a hashed device credential, directories, Wi-Fi network lists, PBX line credentials (stored encrypted) and uploaded diagnostics. It keeps no call history, although its operating logs record call activity.

Your organisation is the Responsible Party for this information and decides who can access it and for how long it is kept; its privacy policy applies to it. Eccentric Badger has no access to it. If your organisation gives us access for support, we act as its Operator and process the information only on its instructions. Please direct questions about information on your organisation’s Server to your administrator.
