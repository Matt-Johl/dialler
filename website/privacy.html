Privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Dialler is made by Eccentric Badger (Pty) Ltd. This policy explains what personal information the Dialler app, the Dialler server and this website handle, and who is responsible for it. It applies the South African Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

In short

  • The Dialler app sends nothing to Eccentric Badger. It connects only to the Dialler server your organisation runs.
  • Your call history stays on your phone.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Your organisation is responsible for the information held on its Dialler server.

The Dialler app

Stored on your phone

  • Your enrolment: the server address, your extension, a device credential (kept in the iOS keychain) and the server's certificate fingerprint.
  • Your directory and favourites.
  • Your call history.
  • The Wi-Fi networks on which the phone receives calls.

Signing out removes your enrolment, directory and Wi-Fi settings. Deleting the app removes everything, including call history.

Sent to your organisation's Dialler server

  • Calls: your device identifier and extension, the numbers you call and that call you, and call audio. Audio is encrypted and relayed to the other party; Dialler does not record calls.
  • Directory: changes you make to your contacts and favourites.
  • Diagnostics: app logs and crash and performance reports from iOS, sent automatically when the app starts. Credentials are removed from logs before sending. The server keeps diagnostics for 30 days by default.

Every connection is encrypted and made only to your organisation's server, whose certificate the app checks against the one it recorded at enrolment.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to carry your voice on calls.
  • Camera: to read the enrolment QR code. No images are stored or sent.
  • Local network: to reach your organisation's server.

Demo mode

In demo mode, calls are simulated on the phone and nothing is sent anywhere.

The Dialler server

Your organisation installs and runs the Dialler server on its own infrastructure. The server holds each phone's extension and label, a hashed device credential, directories, Wi-Fi network lists, PBX line credentials (stored encrypted) and uploaded diagnostics. It keeps no call history, although its operating logs record call activity.

Your organisation is the responsible party for this information and decides who can access it and for how long it is kept. Eccentric Badger has no access to it. If your organisation gives us access for support, we act as its operator and process the information only on its instructions. Please direct questions about information on your organisation's server to your administrator.

We share it only with service providers who work for us under contract and may not use it for their own purposes, with our professional advisers, with authorities where the law requires, and with a prospective buyer or merger partner. Where information is transferred outside South Africa, we ensure it is protected to a standard comparable to POPIA, by law or by contract.

We keep it for as long as these purposes require and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. You can opt out of marketing at any time by using the link in any message or by emailing us.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. If you use the light and dark switch, your choice is saved in your browser only. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, for security and operation.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Dialler itself encrypts signalling (TLS) and call audio (SRTP) between phone and server. If a breach occurs, we will notify those affected and the Information Regulator as the law requires.
